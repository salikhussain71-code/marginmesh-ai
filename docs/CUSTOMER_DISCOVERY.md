# Customer discovery guide

Purpose: learn whether export finance teams really have this problem **before** building more.
Do not pitch savings. Do not ask for confidential documents in the first conversation.

## Who to talk to

Finance managers, cost accountants, export / merchandising managers and owners at textile and
apparel exporters. Public starting points: TDAP (tdap.gov.pk), PTEA (ptea.org.pk), LinkedIn.

## Interview questions

1. Walk me through how you calculate the expected profit of an export order.
2. How do you compare expected and actual cost after production?
3. How do you check supplier invoices against purchase orders? How long does it take?
4. Which discrepancies keep recurring (price, quantity, extra items, freight, deductions)?
5. How do you detect buyer deductions or short payments? What happens next?
6. Which tools do you use today (Excel, ERP, other)?
7. What would you need to see to trust an automated report?
8. Would you test a tool on anonymised sample records? Who decides?

## Decision gate

Continue only if several people independently describe the same costly problem and at least one
is willing to test with anonymised data. Otherwise change the problem or the customer.
